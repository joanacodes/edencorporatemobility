Solutions built for your sector

Housing a consultant, a construction crew or an expatriate executive are three different jobs. Here is how we work with each.

  • Consulting firmsA consultant on a six-month engagement needs a real apartment near the client, not a hotel room.
  • Construction companiesCrews working far from home must be housed properly, close to the site and on time. We take that off your hands.
  • NGOs and organisationsShort notice, tight budgets, dates that move: we handle the unexpected and give you clean paperwork.
  • SMEs and mid-size companiesNo travel manager in-house? We play that role for your Paris trips, with one contact and one quote.
  • International groupsSeconding executives or opening a Paris office: housing, transfers, family settling-in, all in English.
