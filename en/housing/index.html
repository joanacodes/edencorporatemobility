Ready-to-live-in housing for every employee, in Paris and the surrounding region

You tell us who is coming, when, where they will work and with what budget. We select, book and look after the housing for the whole assignment. Your employee arrives; the keys are waiting.

Housing decides whether an assignment succeeds

An employee sent to Paris spends more time in their housing than anywhere else on the assignment. If they sleep badly, spend an hour getting to the site, have nowhere to cook and no table to work at, the assignment suffers, and so does their willingness to go again. A quiet, well-located, properly equipped apartment turns the same assignment into a positive experience they will tell colleagues about.

That is why we do not forward lists of listings. We select properties we know, suited to the length of stay, the work location and the person: a consultant alone for six months, a crew of eight for three weeks, a family settling in for two years. Each case calls for a different format, and we propose the one that fits the budget.

Three formats, depending on the length and rhythm of the assignment

We work with all three families of professional housing. The choice depends on duration, work location, number of people and your budget.

Furnished apartment

For stays of a month or more. A real home with an equipped kitchen, a workspace, a washing machine and peace and quiet. The format for consultants, relocated executives and expatriates. The contract is in your company's name, for a term matched to the assignment.

Aparthotel

For stays of a few weeks. The comfort of an apartment with hotel services: regular cleaning, reception, linen provided, late check-in possible. Practical for crew rotations and assignments whose end date keeps moving.

Hotel

For short stays and last-minute arrivals. We book with establishments we know, close to your work location, at conditions negotiated for repeat stays. Breakfast and flexible cancellation where possible.

Where to house your employees in the Paris region

The right neighbourhood is not the prettiest one: it is the one from which the workplace is reached without thinking about it. We start from the work address and your employee's real hours.

  • Central Paris for assignments in the business districts of the centre and west, favouring areas well served by the metro.
  • La Défense, Neuilly, Boulogne, Issy for group headquarters and consulting engagements.
  • Saint-Denis, Plaine Commune, Roissy for construction sites, logistics and airport-related work.
  • Marne-la-Vallée, Créteil, Massy and the Saclay plateau for industrial, technical and research projects.

For a team we favour one building or one area, so everyone has the same commute and can meet up in the evening.

For groups and teams

Several people on the same project or site? We house teams together, in shared apartments with private bedrooms, in a residence or in a hotel, depending on the budget and your sector's accommodation rules.

We manage staggered arrivals, mid-assignment replacements and extensions. You have a single, up-to-date list of who is housed where and until when. The earlier you tell us, the more choice we have; but we can also absorb an arrival decided the day before.

What we check before proposing a property

A property is not a listing. We choose it as if it were for a member of our own team.

Location
Real travel time to the workplace, by public transport or car, at the hours your employee will actually commute. And nearby shops for daily life.
Quiet and comfort
Bedding, insulation, light, space to work. The difference between a stay endured and a stay enjoyed.
Equipment
Full kitchen, washing machine, reliable wifi, TV, household linen. Everything needed to live, not just to sleep.
Terms
Duration, flexibility, cleaning included or not, deposit, inventory: everything in writing before booking.
The owner or operator
We work with responsive people who can act fast if a boiler breaks down on a Sunday.

What is included

Depending on the format, the quote states what is included: rent or nightly rate, utilities, linen, check-in and check-out cleaning, regular cleaning for aparthotels and hotels, wifi, tourist tax where applicable. It also states what remains payable by the occupant, such as extra consumption or damage. There are no hidden fees: if something is not in the quote, it is not invoiced.

For long stays we can add wellbeing services (welcome, concierge, sport, family support) and organise transport, coordinated with the housing and invoiced on the same document.

Housing questions

Who signs: the company or the employee?

In most cases the booking is made in your company's name; the company remains our contractual contact and receives the invoices. Your employee pays nothing up front and signs nothing on site except the check-in inventory.

Is there a minimum stay?

Not really: we house people for a few nights in a hotel or for several years in an apartment. Under a month, a hotel or aparthotel is usually better suited than a furnished apartment.

What if the assignment runs longer, or ends early?

Tell us, and we extend the stay or arrange a follow-on solution. If it ends early, the notice terms stated in the quote apply; we negotiate them to be as flexible as possible from the start.

Can you house a family?

Yes. For an expatriation or a relocation we look for housing that suits the family: number of bedrooms, neighbourhood, proximity to schools and nurseries, and we support the settling-in.

Do you inspect the properties?

We select properties we know and check each one before your employee arrives. If anything differs from what was agreed, we fix it before they unpack.
